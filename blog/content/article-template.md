# Working title

One-sentence thesis. State the claim, not the topic.

## The problem

What real situation makes this worth understanding? Define the constraints and
the stakes without inflating either.

## What changed my mind

Present the evidence, observation, or production experience that challenged the
obvious answer. Separate firsthand knowledge from inference.

## The mechanism

Explain how the system or idea actually works. Use diagrams, code, or measured
examples only when they make the mechanism clearer.

## What this changes in practice

Turn the argument into decisions a reader can apply. Include tradeoffs and the
conditions under which the recommendation does not hold.

## Open questions

Name what remains uncertain, what would falsify the thesis, and what deserves
further research.

## Sources and acknowledgements

List primary sources, direct references, and any people whose public work
materially informed the note. Do not include private context without explicit
permission.
